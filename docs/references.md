# Musical and implementation references

These support the factual tuning and theory conventions. The explanatory copy
and implementation in Scales are original.

- Standard guitar tuning and interval relationships:
  https://www.fender.com/articles/setup/standard-tuning-how-eadgbe-came-to-be
- Common alternate guitar tunings:
  https://www.fender.com/articles/setup/alternate-guitar-tuning
- Seven-string guitar standard tuning (Ibanez maintenance manual, string 1 to 7):
  https://files.kraftmusic.com/media/ownersmanual/Ibanez_Guitar_Maintenance_Manual.pdf
- Seven-string drop A (SIT Strings):
  https://sitstrings.com/pages/help-center/what-is-drop-tuning-on-a-7-string-guitar
- Standard four-, five-, and six-string bass pitches:
  https://www.studybass.com/gear/bass-tuning/bass-guitar-tunings/
- Diatonic chords and Roman-numeral analysis:
  https://www.studybass.com/lessons/harmony/what-are-major-scale-diatonic-chords/
- Chord tones in bass lines:
  https://www.studybass.com/lessons/harmony/chord-tones-in-basslines/
- E harmonic minor and the raised seventh:
  https://www.fender.com/articles/scales/harmonic-minor-guitar-scale
- Scale structures and mode terminology:
  https://viva.pressbooks.pub/openmusictheory/chapter/diatonic-modes/
- Scale-collection reference and names (names vary by source and tradition):
  https://ianring.com/musictheory/scales/
- Guitar/bass standard pitch uses scientific pitch notation, with middle C = C4.
  The PDF guides show sounding pitches rather than the octave transposition
  commonly used in notation for these instruments.

The tuning list is curated, not a popularity ranking. Lowered and drop variants
are specified by their actual open pitches. The melodic minor catalog uses the
ascending/jazz form in both directions; it does not imply classical descending
natural minor. Modal and non-diatonic progressions can require choices beyond
one key-scale label; the guides emphasize listening and chord tones.

## Audio synthesis

- Jaffe and Smith, _Extensions of the Karplus–Strong Plucked-String Algorithm_:
  https://musicweb.ucsd.edu/~trsmyth/papers/KSExtensions.pdf
- Stanford explanation of fractional tuning and damping:
  https://theory.stanford.edu/~blynn/sound/karplusstrong.html
- Web Audio buffer playback, gain automation, and scheduled sources:
  https://www.w3.org/TR/webaudio/

The synthesis code and excitation buffers are generated by Scales; no recorded
instrument samples or additional sample licenses are used.
