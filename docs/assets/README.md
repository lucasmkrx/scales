# Tukany fretboard screenshots and printable scale charts

Open the English explorer in C major to try the setup pictured below. Changing
the tuning or handedness recalculates the fretboard and any PDF you export.
[Open C major on guitar](https://tukany.com/?key=C)

## Current homepage

- [Light desktop](desktop.png) shows C major on a right-handed, six-string guitar in E standard. C is selected on the circle of fifths. The formula below the fretboard title pairs the seven degrees with their notes. The 21-fret neck shows all five CAGED patterns, and the treble staff covers the pitches they use. These desktop captures predate the one-page chart button and paper-size selector.
- [Dark desktop](desktop-dark.png) shows the same setup and layout after switching themes. Both full-page captures measure 1360 × 2169 pixels.

## Printable output

- [C major on left-handed guitar](pdf-reference.png) shows the one-page A4 practice chart. It includes the note and degree formula, full neck, treble staff, and five compact CAGED positions.

## Earlier interface

The mobile screenshots and walkthrough document the previous homepage with its 0–12 view and PDF settings. The phone captures and walkthrough used build `c4a0041d84eb`, apart from the scale picker as noted below.

- `mobile.png` shows a right-handed, six-string guitar in E standard with C major selected at 390 × 1880 CSS pixels, from the page header
  through the stacked settings and fretboard. The fixed-size 0–12 diagram
  and its pattern brackets scroll horizontally together to the selected pattern.
  The root legend and Copy link share a footer above the horizontal scroll hint.
  A sticky current-scale shortcut provides a direct route from settings to the
  fretboard; the selected pattern remains visible when the viewport changes.
- `mobile-dark.png` shows the same 390 × 1880 phone layout in Dark, including
  the appearance control, instrument icons, and fixed-size neck.
  Switching appearance preserves the measured page positions.
- `mobile-picker.png` shows the Pentatonic family at 390 × 860 CSS pixels in build `7af9dc2c6224`.
  The card and sticky shortcut still show C major while the player browses.
- `walkthrough.gif` opens with C major. The player browses the Pentatonic
  family, chooses A as the root and Minor pentatonic as the scale, and selects
  its first two-notes-per-string shape. The full-neck view then shows Pattern 2
  in degrees. The last frame clears the pattern, includes all 12 root notes in
  the PDF settings, and switches to Dark. The five frames
  come from a local Chromium session using the default right-handed orientation. They
  are resized to 1020 × 1170 pixels with a shared color palette. The desktop frame
  height accommodates the longer full-neck layout, so the initial 0–12 view has
  some extra space below its content.
